One idea connects all five chapters

Chapter 1 · What and why

An investment is defined by its purpose. Suitability depends on the investor's goals, horizon, liquidity needs and capacity for loss.

Chapter 2 · The machinery

Markets create, sell and trade securities. Market direction + position structure = investor outcome: long, margin and short differ.

Chapter 3 · Information and execution

Good investing needs reliable information, the right benchmark and the right order.

Chapters 4 and 5 · Return vs risk

Money has a date (TVM). Is the return enough for the risk? For a portfolio, only systematic risk is priced.

The fastest study loop

4. Write
