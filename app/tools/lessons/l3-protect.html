THE BIG STORY

Every trade starts in a hole

Buy RM10,000 of shares and pay RM50 in total costs to get in and out: the investment must earn RM50 before you make a real profit. Transaction costs are a hurdle rate. This lesson covers that hurdle, what investor protection actually protects, and what advisors and investment clubs can and cannot do.

Net return is what counts

Fixed commissions are typical on small trades; negotiated commissions on large ones. A strategy that is profitable before costs can be unattractive after them, especially high-frequency trading.

Gross return − costs = net return

Investor protection: what it covers

Securities Investor Protection Corporation

Protects against the financial failure of a broker; the slides cite historical limits of USD 500,000 for securities and USD 250,000 for cash.

It does not guarantee against churning, bad advice or ordinary market losses. A bad investment decision remains your risk.

Mediation = negotiate

An informal, voluntary dispute-resolution process between a customer and a broker; non-binding if the parties cannot agree.

Arbitration = adjudicate

A more formal process in which customer and broker present arguments to a panel; binding arbitration means accepting the panel's decision and giving up the right to sue.

Investment advisors

  • Must register with the SEC, but no law or regulator guarantees competence
  • Look for experience and designations: CFA, CFP, CPA and others
  • Typical fees: 2–3% a year of assets for small portfolios, 0.25–0.75% for large ones
  • A good advisor asks you lots of questions first

Investment clubs

  • A legal partnership where members pool money and knowledge
  • Members recommend stocks and analyse performance together
  • Benefits: shared research, education, pooled resources
  • Risk: group confidence can exceed group competence

CALCULATE

Buy 1,000 shares at RM5.00, sell at RM5.30, RM30 commission each way

Gross and net profit?

Gross RM300; commissions RM60; net RM240. Net return 240 / (5,000 + 30) ≈ 4.77% rather than the gross 6%.
EVALUATE

"My broker is SIPC-protected, so my investments can't lose value."

Agree?

No. SIPC covers certain losses from a broker's financial failure, not market declines, bad advice or churning.
EVALUATE

"A registered advisor removes investment risk."

Agree?

No. Registration means meeting regulatory requirements, not guaranteed skill. An advisor can improve the process (planning, diversification, discipline) but cannot eliminate market risk.

Exam lens

  • Transaction costs create a hurdle; frequent trading raises it.
  • SIPC = protection against broker failure, not against losses or bad advice.
  • Mediation: informal and non-binding. Arbitration: formal; binding if agreed.
  • Licensed ≠ competent; a good advisor understands your goals before recommending products.

Quick check

A customer and broker try an informal, voluntary process with a neutral third party, and walk away without agreement. What was it, and is the customer bound by anything?

Mediation, which is non-binding when no agreement is reached. Binding arbitration would have required accepting the panel's decision.
