THE BIG STORY

You want to sell 100 shares. Who is on the other side?

In a broker market, a broker finds another investor and the two of you trade with each other. In a dealer market, a market maker is the other side: it buys from you and later sells to someone else. The cleanest summary: broker = agent, dealer = principal.

{{vis:l2_broker_dealer}}

Broker market

  • Buyer and seller are brought together and transact with each other
  • The broker matches; it carries no inventory
  • Securities exchanges such as the NYSE

Dealer market

  • A market maker stands in the middle and trades on its own account
  • It carries inventory risk and supplies liquidity
  • No central trading floor needed: Nasdaq and the OTC market

Bid and ask: read the quote from the dealer's side

Quote: 99.50 / 100.00.

Bid = the highest price the dealer offers to BUY

If you sell, the dealer buys from you at the bid: you receive 99.50.

Ask = the lowest price the dealer will SELL at

If you buy, the dealer sells to you at the ask: you pay 100.00.

Common mistake: "ask = what the investor asks". No: the dealer asks.

Bid–ask spread = ask − bid = 0.50

Bid < ask, always.

The spread compensates the dealer for providing liquidity and carrying inventory risk. Buying and immediately reselling at unchanged quotes costs you the spread, so it is a transaction cost.

The trading landscape

Exchanges

NYSE (largest, strictest listing), NYSE Amex, regional, options and futures exchanges.

Nasdaq and OTC

Dealer networks connected electronically; OTC Bulletin Board and Pink Sheets list smaller, riskier firms.

Third market

Institutions trade exchange-listed stocks through market makers that are not exchange members, at lower cost.

Fourth market

Institutions trade directly with each other through ECNs, bypassing market makers.

Why bypass?

Cost. 0.10% on a RM500 million trade is RM500,000.

A market is a mechanism

Not necessarily a place: once orders meet electronically, no floor is needed.

APPLY

A dealer quotes RM9.80 – RM10.00

What do you receive if you sell, and pay if you buy?

Sell: RM9.80 (the bid). Buy: RM10.00 (the ask). The RM0.20 spread is the dealer's compensation and your round-trip cost.
EVALUATE

"A dealer is basically another name for a broker."

Agree or disagree?

Wrong. A broker facilitates a trade between two parties; a dealer is itself a counterparty. That difference drives inventory risk, liquidity provision and bid–ask quotes.
CLASSIFY

Two pension funds trade a block of shares directly via an ECN

Third or fourth market?

Fourth market: institutions deal directly with each other, bypassing market makers. If a non-member market maker were involved, it would be the third market.

Exam lens

  • Broker = agent (matches); dealer = principal (trades, holds inventory).
  • Bid = dealer buys; ask = dealer sells; bid < ask.
  • Third market: non-member market makers. Fourth market: institutions direct, often via ECNs.

Quick check

You buy 1,000 shares at the ask of RM5.05 and immediately sell them at the bid of RM5.00. What did the round trip cost you, ignoring commissions?

1,000 × (5.05 − 5.00) = RM50: the bid–ask spread.
